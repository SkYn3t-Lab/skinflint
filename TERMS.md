skinflint shortens what Claude reads and writes. It can therefore remove
text you would have wanted, although it is built to keep error lines and to
save the full text of anything it trims. Check its work as you would any
tool's, and switch it off (`stop skinflint`) whenever you want Claude's
unshortened output.
